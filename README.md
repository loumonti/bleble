# topo vs. águila 
### a game made for campfire and polished for horizons & sleepover - by tats (@beesfan) and @monti

---------
## what is this?
this is a fun runner/platformer game in which you are being chased by an eagle and jump to avoid enemies/obstacles to escape! we originally made it
for campfire in bogotá, but since it was VERY buggy when shipped, we decided to polish it and keep on working on it for other ysws!

## what does it have?

- 3 levels! all with their own backgrounds and level designed (drawn by monti, ofc)
- a start screen, level select, game over, and level completed screen (with custom buttons :p)
- 2 types of enemies (rats and snakes) and 2 types of obstacles (spikes and fire)
- an eagle that chases the player!! (yes we know it gets stuck at some point level 1, ignore it shhh)

## why?
this was monti's first game in godot! it actually turned out pretty fire, and it was the first time she used aseprite too!
overall a huge learning experience; we learned to work as a team, to ship a project within a VERY tight timeframe and to make a runner/platformer-type
game in godot.

## ORIGINAL README

Imaginate ser un topo, un topo muy tierno pero que los depredadores de la superficie te cazen :(
Bueno de eso es este juego, Topo VS Aguila, un projecto de campfire con la tempatica de Under the Surface. Nuestro topito es el main que representa el tema, donde los niveles suceden debajo de la tierra con
la Aguila, serpientes y ratas persiguiendote. Es un juego hecho en Godot, con el arte y codigo hechos totalmente a mano (bueno no literalmente).

Hecho en 2 dias con mucho estres y mucho gusto :)
-Monti y Tats
